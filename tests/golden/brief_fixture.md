## Annex

### A. Confidence and method

- Forward test: fixture rule; 3 rolling windows, 40 KEV additions. Model 5 vs EPSS 5 in the top 100 (+3, 95% CI +1 to +6).
- Against it: EPSS mean average precision 0.0200 vs 0.0200 for the model; at the top 1% the difference is +3 (95% CI -2 to +4).
- Confidence key, on the number of positive events a judgement rests on: low under 10; moderate under 50 or when another measure points the other way; otherwise high. A confidence label never shares a sentence with a likelihood word.

### B. Limitations

From the export's own disclosures, verbatim:

- SYNTHETIC FIXTURE: every number on this dashboard is made up.
- Scores only CVEs published from 2024-01-01 that were not in CISA KEV at the snapshot; the queue is the critical, high and elevated tiers.
- Rank and tier only: the model's output is not a probability (fit at 0.147% prevalence; snapshot EPSS is a newer model version than training saw).
- Snapshot EPSS is model v-fixture; the risk model was trained on v-fixture. Percentiles are used, but the shift is untested.
- The snapshot ranking itself is not evaluated. Tier hit rates are historical: the same procedure on the rolling-origin test folds (2025-05-04 to 2026-10-26), next 180 days.
- Newer CVEs rank higher (standardized log-age weight -0.50): 0 of the 119 CVEs in the queue are 30 days old or younger.
- Weights describe correlated signals, not causes; the refit's weights differ from the evaluated folds' (both shown).
- CVSS, CWE, assigner and descriptions are context only and were never model inputs.
- The logistic model (pre-registered headline) ranks the snapshot, not the boosting challenger: it was fixed before scoring and its contributions can be explained.
